# Job Search AI Operating System — screenshots (v1.4.0)

Captured 2026-09-28 on claude.ai (light theme, 2× then scaled down) with the v1.4.0 plugin and a
made-up job seeker: **Priya Nair, RN** (hourly, $44/hour minimum). Every employer, posting, offer,
connection and number is fictional.

Each image comes in two sizes: `<name>.png` (1600 px wide) and `<name>-800.png` (800 px wide
thumbnail).

## Install

| File | What it shows | Suggested caption |
|---|---|---|
| `01-install-add-menu` | Customize → Plugins, the **Add** menu open: Add marketplace, Upload plugin, Create a plugin, Create with Claude. | In the Claude app, open Customize → Plugins and click **Add**. |
| `02-install-add-marketplace` | The Add marketplace dialog: Browse Anthropic sources or **Add from a repository**. | Choose **Add from a repository**. |
| `03-install-repository` | The repository field filled with `alexclowe/job-search-os`, **Sync automatically** on, and the **Sync** button. | Type `alexclowe/job-search-os` and click **Sync**. Leave Sync automatically on to get new releases. |
| `04-install-discover-add` | Discover, filtered to the new marketplace: Job Search AI Operating System with an **Add** button. | The plugin shows up under Discover. Click **Add**. |
| `05-install-plugin-details` | The installed plugin's page: by The AI Career Lab · 1.4.0 · 38 skills, the description, and the on switch. | Installed: version 1.4.0, 38 skills, and it only adds instructions for Claude. |
| `06-install-upload-zip` | Upload a plugin, with `job-search-os-claude-plugin-v1.4.zip` chosen and its preview. | Or upload the zip from the free download: Add → Upload plugin → Upload. |

## The five jobs

| File | What it shows | Suggested caption |
|---|---|---|
| `10-check-card` | Tailor & apply's check card for a Brightline Health charge nurse posting: no caution signals, pay $40–$46 against her $44 minimum, strong fit with two gaps, two people she knows there. Then the three choices, and the intro request to Marcus drafted after "Get the intro first". | One check card before any tailoring: is it real, the pay, the fit, who you know. Then tailor it, skip it, or get the intro first. |
| `11-tailored-output` | Tailor & apply for a Maplewood Family Health posting: "Has Priya done these?" questions, her answer, and the resume, cover letter and form answers updated only after she confirms. | It asks before it adds anything your record doesn't show. |
| `12-application-board` | The Application Board: a column per stage (Interested, Applied, Screening, Interviewing, Onsite, Offer, Closed), every card with its stage in words and a Stage menu, and "Due this week". | Your Application Board: move a card, and your next job picks it up. |
| `13-search-funnel` | The Search Funnel from the funnel report: applied → got a reply → screen → interview → final round → offer, as bars, for all sources and each source, with "too few to tell yet" for small sources. | Where your search stalls, by source. |
| `14-offer-comparison` | The Offer Comparison: two hourly offers side by side (base, differentials and overtime, schedule, benefits, first-year total, deadline, salary check). "Not stated" where the company didn't say. | Two offers, side by side, hour for hour. |
| `16-practice-round` | The Practice Round view in a mock interview: the current question with an answer timer, the Story Bank meter, and the answered question with its rating and feedback. | Practice out loud, one question at a time. |
| `17-people` | The People board from a LinkedIn connections export: only people at companies on her Application Board, grouped by company, with how she knows them and whether she's asked. | Who you know at the companies you're applying to. It never messages anyone. |
| `18-company-brief` | Interview prep for a fictional employer: the company brief says it has no facts yet and left those sections blank instead of guessing; the prep brief, Story Bank and brief file. | Company briefs use sources or say nothing. Here, a made-up employer, so it left the facts blank. |
| `19-nurse-guide-in-use` | The mock interview set up the way nurses are interviewed ("three are behavioral and one is a clinic scenario"), then feedback on her first answer: what worked, structure, holds up, undersold, length. | Built-in guides for 108 professions: a nurse gets a clinic scenario; feedback in five short lines. |
