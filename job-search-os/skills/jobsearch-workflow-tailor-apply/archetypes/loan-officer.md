# Loan Officer — job-search archetype

**Slug:** `loan-officer` · **Aliases:** mortgage loan originator (MLO), mortgage banker, mortgage advisor, loan originator, mortgage broker (adjacent)
**Also read:** `mortgage-broker` (if present)

## Target titles (and the adjacent ones)
- Loan Officer / Mortgage Loan Originator (retail bank, independent mortgage bank, credit union, broker shop), Senior Loan Officer, Producing Branch Manager, Consumer or Commercial Loan Officer, Loan Officer Assistant (entry), Processor or Underwriter (adjacent paths)
- Adjacent worth searching: Mortgage Sales Manager, Wholesale Account Executive, Business Development Officer, Retention/Recapture Specialist

## Where the postings live
- Lender and bank career pages, mortgage-industry job boards and associations, recruiter-driven placement (common for producing LOs), and the general boards; many moves happen through direct recruiting based on production rather than postings
- Broker shops and small IMBs often hire on relationship and a book of business

## What a recruiter screens for first
- NMLS licensing status and state licences (or federal registration for depository banks), production history (volume and units, verified against NMLS and the company's records), purchase-versus-refinance mix, referral sources (realtors, builders), and clean compliance history
- Ask how production is verified before you state it; the numbers will be checked

## Story types that land
- A deal saved: what went wrong in the file, what you did with the borrower, agent, and underwriter
- How you built a referral relationship and what it produces (in relative terms if the figure is confidential)
- A compliance-sensitive moment handled correctly (disclosures, fair-lending, a difficult denial)
- Pipeline discipline: how you keep three audiences informed on the same loan

## Resume conventions for this profession
- NMLS number where customary; production by year in units and volume only if you can substantiate it; product mix; systems (LOS by name, CRM); licences by state
- Avoid inflating production or claiming team numbers as your own; "closed" means funded in this industry

## Red flags in postings
- 100% commission with no leads, no marketing support, and a non-compete; "must bring your own book" with an aggressive draw clawback; compensation described only as "unlimited"

## Verify before relying on this
- NMLS Consumer Access and your state regulator for licensing; the lender's own comp plan document; industry associations for current market norms
