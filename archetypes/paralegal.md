# Paralegal — job-search archetype

**Slug:** `paralegal` · **Aliases:** legal assistant, litigation paralegal, corporate paralegal, legal secretary (adjacent), law clerk (adjacent)
**Also read:** none

## Target titles (and the adjacent ones)
- Paralegal with practice area (Litigation, Corporate/Transactional, Real Estate, Immigration, Family, IP, Estate Planning), Senior Paralegal, Paralegal Manager, Legal Assistant, Litigation Support Specialist, E-Discovery Specialist, Contracts Administrator
- Adjacent worth searching: Compliance Analyst, Legal Operations Specialist, Contract Manager, Docket Clerk, Claims Examiner

## Where the postings live
- Law-firm career pages, legal-industry job boards and paralegal associations, legal staffing agencies, in-house legal departments via the general boards, and government and court job systems
- Firms often hire through staffing agencies for temp-to-perm; associations' boards carry the specialised roles

## What a recruiter screens for first
- Practice-area match, years in that practice area, the systems named in the posting (document management, e-discovery platforms, e-filing, billing software), certificate or degree (ABA-approved program, NALA/NFPA credentials where valued), and writing sample quality
- Confidentiality and conflicts questions come early; be ready to describe your work without naming clients

## Story types that land
- A filing, deadline, or production you owned end to end under pressure
- A document set you organised that changed how the team worked
- A drafting task where the attorney's edits were minimal, and why
- A client or opposing-counsel interaction you handled with care

## Resume conventions for this profession
- Practice areas and systems near the top; per role: matter types, volume in relative terms, what you drafted versus what you supported; certifications listed with the issuing body
- Never name clients or confidential matters; describe by type. "Assisted attorneys" is filler; say what you produced

## Red flags in postings
- Paralegal title with legal-secretary duties and pay, billable-hour targets without support, "notary required" as the main qualification, practice areas listed as "all"

## Verify before relying on this
- Your state's rules on paralegal practice and unauthorised practice of law; NALA and NFPA for credentials; the firm's own posting for systems and practice specifics
